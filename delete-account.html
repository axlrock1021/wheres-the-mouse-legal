鼠鼠王國 刪除帳號說明

更新 2026-10-08

English version below ↓

在遊戲裡刪除（建議）

  1. 打開遊戲、登入你要刪除的帳號。
  2. 大廳右側 → 設定（木牌）→ 帳號 區塊 → 刪除帳號。
  3. 第一次確認：按「刪除帳號」。
  4. 第二次確認：輸入「刪除」兩個字（英文介面輸入 DELETE），再按「刪除帳號」。
  5. 看到「帳號刪掉了」就完成了，遊戲會回到登入畫面。

無法進入遊戲時：用信箱申請

寄信到 andy10210910@gmail.com，標題「刪除帳號」，內容請附：

  • 你的登入方式（訪客／Google／Facebook）。
  • 遊戲內的暱稱；用 Google／Facebook 登入的請從該帳號的信箱寄出，方便我們確認是本人。
  • 訪客帳號因為沒有任何可以識別你的資訊，只能從遊戲內刪除（或直接移除遊戲，進度就留在那支手機上不會再被使用）。

我們會在 7 個工作天內處理並回信。

刪除後會發生什麼

刪除帳號、暱稱、頭像、關卡進度與星數、起司幣與鑽石、道具與貴重品、圖鑑、簽到、任務、成就、卡片、禮包、設定、登入紀錄、裝置／第三方帳號識別碼，以及你送出的抓蟲蟲／許願池回報（文字、截圖、手機型號等）。
去識別化保留真實貨幣的購買紀錄（訂單編號、商品、金額、時間）與相關帳目——移除帳號與裝置識別碼、暱稱，無法再連回你；用於法令要求的交易紀錄保存與退款爭議處理。
其他說明已經發布的全服公告（例如改版感謝公告）如果列過你打碼後的暱稱，會維持原樣（打碼後無法連回你）。回報當時寄到開發者信箱的通知信，可以寫信到上面的信箱要求一併刪除。
不能復原刪除是立即且永久的。之後用同一種方式登入會是全新帳號，進度從零開始。

Mouse Kingdom: Where's the Mouse — Delete your account

Updated 2026-10-08

In the Game (recommended)

  1. Open the Game and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Lobby, right side → Settings (the wooden sign) → Account → Delete account.
  3. First confirmation: tap "Delete account".
  4. Second confirmation: type DELETE (in Chinese UI type 刪除 / 删除), then tap "Delete account".
  5. When you see "Account deleted" it is done; the Game returns to the sign-in screen.

If you cannot open the Game: request by email

Email andy10210910@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and include:

  • How you signed in (guest / Google / Facebook).
  • Your in-game nickname; for Google/Facebook accounts please send from that account's email address so we can verify it is you.
  • Guest accounts contain nothing that identifies you, so they can only be deleted from inside the Game (or simply uninstall the Game — the progress stays on that phone and is never used again).

We will process the request and reply within 7 working days.

What happens after deletion

DeletedAccount, nickname, avatar, level progress and stars, Cheese coins and Diamonds, items and valuables, collection, check-ins, tasks, achievements, cards, gift packs, settings, login records, device / third-party account identifiers, and the Bug Hunt / Wishing Well reports you sent (text, screenshots, device model, etc.).
Kept, de-identifiedReal-money purchase records (order ID, product, amount, time) and related ledger entries — with account/device identifiers and nickname removed so they can no longer be linked to you; kept for legally required transaction records and refund disputes.
Other notesServer-wide notices already published (e.g. update thank-you notices) that listed your masked nickname stay as they are (a masked nickname cannot be linked back to you). Notification emails sent to the developer's mailbox when you reported can also be deleted on request to the email above.
Not reversibleDeletion is immediate and permanent. Signing in the same way afterwards creates a brand-new account starting from zero.
