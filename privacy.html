鼠鼠王國 隱私權政策

生效日 2026-10-08（草稿；正式上架前會再確認）

English version below ↓

本政策說明 AndyRock（以下稱「我們」）在你使用手機遊戲「鼠鼠王國」（以下稱「本遊戲」）時，會蒐集哪些資料、怎麼使用、怎麼保護，以及你有哪些權利。本遊戲由個人開發者製作。

一、我們蒐集哪些資料

資料什麼時候、為什麼
裝置識別碼（遊戲自己產生的隨機 ID）以「訪客」方式遊玩時，用來認出同一支手機上的同一個帳號、保存進度。
第三方帳號識別碼（Google、Facebook 的使用者 ID）你選擇用 Google 或 Facebook 登入／綁定時，用來認出同一個帳號、跨裝置保存進度。我們只取得識別碼（以及登入服務提供的基本資料，例如顯示名稱），不會取得你的密碼、聯絡人、貼文。
遊戲資料暱稱、頭像、關卡進度、星數、遊戲內貨幣與道具、簽到、任務、成就、設定、手機時區。用來提供遊戲功能。
購買紀錄你透過 Google Play 購買商品時，Google Play 提供的訂單編號與購買憑證。用來發放商品、處理退款、防止重複領取。付款資料（信用卡等）由 Google 處理，我們不會取得。
廣告相關資料本遊戲使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告（獎勵廣告、插頁廣告）。AdMob 可能依其政策取得裝置的廣告識別碼與相關資訊，詳見下方「第三方服務」。
技術資料遊戲版本、作業系統、連線時的 IP 位址與請求紀錄（伺服器日誌，用來除錯、維護與防止濫用）。
意見回饋（抓蟲蟲、許願池）你主動送出回報時：你寫的文字、你從相簿選的截圖（最多 3 張，可以不附）、玩家 ID、遊戲版本、手機型號、系統版本、最後玩的關卡，以及你是否勾選「想被公開表揚」。用來修正問題、評估許願、發放回報獎勵。每筆回報會以通知信（透過 Gmail 寄送）寄到開發者信箱，截圖作為附件。
應用程式內評分遊戲可能請 Google Play 跳出評分視窗。評分由 Google 處理，本遊戲不會知道你有沒有評分、評了幾星。

我們不會要求你提供真實姓名、地址、電話；也不會存取你的麥克風、位置。相簿只有在你送出抓蟲蟲／許願池回報、主動從相簿選截圖時，才會上傳你選的那幾張（最多 3 張）；本遊戲是透過系統的選圖畫面取得那張圖，不會讀取相簿裡其他照片，也不需要「存取相簿」權限。iOS 版目前沒有附截圖的功能。

二、我們怎麼使用這些資料

  • 提供遊戲功能、保存與同步進度。
  • 發放購買的商品、處理退款與補單。
  • 顯示廣告、計算看廣告的獎勵。
  • 維護、除錯、防止作弊與濫用。
  • 依法令要求保存必要的交易紀錄。
  • 處理你送出的意見回饋：修正問題、評估許願、發放回報獎勵、回覆你。

我們不會把你的資料賣給任何人，也不會用於與本遊戲無關的行銷。

三、第三方服務

本遊戲使用下列第三方服務，它們會依各自的隱私權政策處理資料：

  • Google Play 服務、Google Play 結帳（購買）、Google 登入：Google 隱私權政策
  • Google AdMob（廣告）：AdMob 說明、Google 廣告政策
  • Facebook 登入（Meta）：Meta 隱私權政策

你可以在手機系統設定中重設或關閉廣告識別碼的個人化廣告。

四、資料存放

本遊戲的伺服器放在 Google Cloud（新加坡機房），資料庫由 Neon 代管（同樣位於新加坡）。如果你不住在新加坡，你的資料會被傳送到新加坡儲存與處理（跨境傳輸），只用於提供本遊戲的服務。意見回饋的通知信透過 Gmail 寄到開發者信箱。

五、全服公告與公開表揚

抓蟲蟲回報被確認是 bug 時，如果你在送出時勾選「想被公開表揚」，我們可能在遊戲內的全服公告（例如改版感謝公告）列出你的暱稱，但會打碼：保留第一個字和最後一個字、中間換成 xxx（例如「起司鼠1234」顯示成「起xxx4」；兩個字的暱稱只顯示第一個字加 x；一個字的暱稱顯示成「神秘鼠鼠」）。不會顯示你的玩家 ID。沒有勾選就不會列出。

六、資料保存多久

  • 帳號與遊戲資料：保存到你刪除帳號為止。
  • 購買紀錄：刪除帳號後，會以去識別化的方式保留（移除帳號識別碼、裝置識別碼、暱稱；只留訂單編號、商品、金額、時間），以符合法令對交易紀錄的保存要求與處理退款爭議。
  • 伺服器日誌：保存一段合理期間（通常不超過 90 天）後刪除。
  • 意見回饋：回報處理完成後 30 天刪除截圖、60 天刪除整筆（文字與其他資料）。還沒處理、或回報獎勵還沒領的回報會保留到處理完、領完為止，再照上面的天數刪除。開發者信箱裡的通知信由開發者另行管理。刪除帳號時，你的回報與截圖會一起刪除。

七、刪除帳號

你可以隨時在遊戲裡刪除帳號：大廳 → 設定 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號（會請你確認兩次）。刪除後進度、夥伴、頭像、遊戲內貨幣與道具全部消失，無法復原；之後用同一種方式登入會是全新的帳號。無法進入遊戲時，也可以用信箱申請，做法見 刪除帳號說明。

八、你的權利

你可以要求查詢、更正或刪除我們持有的你的資料，或對資料的處理提出疑問。請用下方的聯絡信箱與我們聯繫；我們會在合理期間內回覆。

九、兒童

本遊戲不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。如果你認為我們不小心取得了兒童的資料，請聯繫我們，我們會刪除。

十、安全

資料儲存於伺服器並以合理的技術與管理措施保護（連線加密、存取控管）。但網路傳輸與儲存沒有絕對安全，我們無法保證百分之百不會發生事故。

十一、政策變更

我們可能更新本政策；更新後會在本頁面公告並更新生效日。重大變更會在遊戲內提醒。

Mouse Kingdom: Where's the Mouse — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-08 (draft; to be finalized before store release)

This policy explains what data AndyRock ("we") collects when you play the mobile game "Mouse Kingdom: Where's the Mouse" (the "Game"), how it is used and protected, and your rights. The Game is made by an individual developer.

1. Data we collect

DataWhen and why
Device identifier (a random ID generated by the Game)When you play as a guest, to recognize the same account on the same phone and keep your progress.
Third-party account identifier (Google or Facebook user ID)When you choose to sign in or link with Google or Facebook, to recognize the same account across devices. We receive only the identifier (and basic profile data the sign-in service provides, such as a display name) — never your password, contacts or posts.
Game dataNickname, avatar, level progress, stars, in-game currency and items, check-ins, tasks, achievements, settings, device time zone. Used to provide the Game.
Purchase recordsWhen you buy through Google Play: the order ID and purchase token provided by Google Play. Used to deliver products, handle refunds and prevent duplicate grants. Payment details (cards etc.) are handled by Google; we never receive them.
Advertising dataThe Game shows ads via Google AdMob (rewarded and interstitial). AdMob may collect the device advertising ID and related information under its own policies — see "Third-party services".
Technical dataGame version, OS, IP address and request logs when connecting (server logs, for debugging, maintenance and abuse prevention).
Feedback (Bug Hunt, Wishing Well)When you choose to send a report: the text you write, screenshots you pick from your photo library (up to 3, optional), player ID, game version, device model, OS version, the last level you played, and whether you ticked "public shout-out". Used to fix problems, evaluate wishes and grant report rewards. Each report is emailed (via Gmail) to the developer's mailbox as a notification, with the screenshots attached.
In-app ratingThe Game may ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. Ratings are handled by Google; the Game never learns whether you rated it or how many stars you gave.

We do not ask for your real name, address or phone number, and we do not access your microphone or location. Photos are uploaded only when you send a Bug Hunt / Wishing Well report and pick screenshots yourself (up to 3); the Game receives only the pictures you choose through the system picker, never reads any other photos, and does not need photo-library permission. The iOS version currently has no screenshot feature.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the Game and save/sync progress.
  • To deliver purchases and handle refunds.
  • To show ads and grant ad rewards.
  • Maintenance, debugging, anti-cheat and abuse prevention.
  • To keep transaction records required by law.
  • To handle feedback you send: fixing problems, evaluating wishes, granting report rewards and replying to you.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for marketing unrelated to the Game.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play services, Google Play Billing, Google Sign-In: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads): AdMob help, Google advertising policies
  • Facebook Login (Meta): Meta Privacy Policy

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads via your device's advertising ID settings.

4. Data storage

The Game's servers run on Google Cloud (Singapore region) and the database is hosted by Neon (also in Singapore). If you live outside Singapore, your data is transferred to and stored in Singapore (cross-border transfer), and is used only to provide the Game. Feedback notification emails are sent to the developer's mailbox via Gmail.

5. Server-wide notices and public shout-outs

If a Bug Hunt report is confirmed as a bug and you ticked "public shout-out" when sending it, we may list your nickname in an in-game server-wide notice (for example, an update thank-you notice), masked: the first and last characters are kept and the middle is replaced with xxx (e.g. "Cheese1234" becomes "Cxxx4"; a two-character nickname shows the first character plus x; a one-character nickname shows as "神秘鼠鼠" (Mystery Mouse)). Your player ID is never shown. If you did not tick it, you are not listed.

6. Retention

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: after account deletion they are kept in de-identified form (account ID, device ID and nickname removed; only order ID, product, amount and time remain) to meet legal record-keeping requirements and resolve refund disputes.
  • Server logs: kept for a reasonable period (normally no more than 90 days), then deleted.
  • Feedback: screenshots are deleted 30 days after the report is resolved, and the whole report (text and other data) 60 days after. Reports not yet resolved, or with an unclaimed report reward, are kept until resolved/claimed and then deleted on the same schedule. Notification emails in the developer's mailbox are managed separately by the developer. When you delete your account, your reports and screenshots are deleted with it.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the Game at any time: Lobby → Settings → Account → Delete account (you will be asked to confirm twice). All progress, partners, avatars, in-game currency and items are erased and cannot be restored; signing in the same way afterwards creates a brand-new account. If you cannot open the Game, you can request deletion by email — see Delete account.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or raise questions about how it is processed. Contact us at the email below; we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe we have obtained a child's data by mistake, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is stored on our servers and protected by reasonable technical and organizational measures (encrypted connections, access control). No transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee that incidents will never occur.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date; significant changes will be announced in the Game.
